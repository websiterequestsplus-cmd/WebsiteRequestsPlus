FAQ

Common questions.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the project. Pages, features, and design detail all change the work, so we quote every site individually. Contact sales with your idea and we'll send you a quote.

How long does a project take?

That depends on the size of the site. We'll give you a timeline along with your quote.

Do I need Discord?

No. Discord is the fastest way to talk with us, but you can send your request by email instead.

Will my site work on phones?

Yes. Every site we build is made to work on phones, tablets, and desktops.
